PRODUCTS WE CAN BUILD OR CONFIGURE

Water work, made useful.

These are configurable offerings, not deployed Teregon Water products or claims of measured impact. Each begins with a real workflow and the records available to support it.

01

GROUNDED PRODUCT SUPPORT

Answers from the right manual.

A customer or technician asks about an installation, filter change, appliance fault, or service policy. A support assistant searches approved manuals, product guides, and policies, then drafts an answer tied to its source.

  • Retrieval across product and service knowledge
  • Source-linked answers and clear uncertainty
  • Escalation when evidence is missing
02

SERVICE + CASE TRIAGE

Move a service case forward.

When a case arrives, combine the customer’s description with product records and approved service evidence. Classify the issue and prepare a next step; deterministic rules and a person review any consequential update.

  • Issue classification and evidence retrieval
  • Parts, warranty, and policy checks when configured
  • Human approval before record changes
03

SUPPORT SIGNALS + QUALITY

See recurring questions sooner.

Bring support cases, service notes, and product feedback into a shared view. Group recurring questions and themes so service and product teams can investigate patterns against the source records.

  • Recurring issue and contact themes
  • Traceable paths from pattern to source case
  • Baselines agreed before impact is measured
04

WATERSHED SYSTEMS

Connect upstream and downstream.

For planning work, combine available rainfall, stream observations, land cover, and community assets into shared watershed context. Use the data and boundaries agreed with the project team; this is not a live sensor network.

  • Watershed and river context views
  • Bring existing observations into one place
  • Scenario exploration for human-led planning
