BRIOBOT / SUPPORT OPERATIONS

BrioBot case triage

From a Salesforce case to a review-ready action.

Company-level ARR context reported by the team: approximately $240K at the time. This revenue is not attributed to BrioBot.

For e-commerce support, a LangGraph flow gathers case context, retrieves product and policy evidence, reads attached images, and drafts structured actions. Deterministic Python gates validate writable fields; dry-run and safe-stop paths keep Salesforce updates explicit, with Langfuse traces for review.

BRIOPEDIA / KNOWLEDGE SYSTEMS
