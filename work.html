WHAT TEREGON DOES / 01

Make messy data
useful.

Teregon Labs builds applied AI and software for complex workflows. Teregon Water applies that engineering to water filtration and appliance businesses, water operations, and watershed systems.

FIELD STUDY / NORTH FORK
01

APPLIED AI

Put the right context in reach.

Build AI workflows that search trusted knowledge, triage requests, summarize complex records, and help teams choose the next action—with people in control.

  • Grounded knowledge search
  • Support and operations automation
  • Human review and evidence gates
02

DATA + DECISION SYSTEMS

Connect the pieces.

Bring scattered sources into a dependable view, from CRM and product data to service records, river observations, and watershed information.

  • Data integration and modeling
  • Forecasts tied to customer signals
  • Decision-ready dashboards and tools
03

SOFTWARE ENGINEERING

Ship tools that hold up.

Build focused software around real workflows: clear interfaces, reliable data paths, and AI features with sensible boundaries.

  • Product prototyping and delivery
  • Integrations and internal tools
  • Evaluation, observability, and iteration
04

TEREGON WATER / WATER INTELLIGENCE

Software for water in motion.

Build practical tools for filtration and appliance businesses, service teams, and watershed decisions. Connect manuals, cases, product records, and water observations when those sources are available.

  • Grounded support for water products
  • Evidence-gated service triage
  • Watershed context and planning views
