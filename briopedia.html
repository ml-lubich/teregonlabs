THE CONTEXT

Good support depends on knowledge people can find—and keep current.

Support teams work across manuals, troubleshooting notes, and product guidance. Briopedia was built as an internal knowledge system: a versioned wiki for maintaining material, plus retrieval and chat for bringing relevant sources into a question.

This is prior Brio team product experience. It is not a Teregon client engagement, and no business outcome is attributed here.
